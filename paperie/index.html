繁體中文 / ABOUT

把日常留在像真紙一樣的手帳裡。

Asympta Paperie 是適用於 iPhone 與 iPad 的數位日記與拼貼手帳。可用 Apple Pencil 手寫、翻動紙頁、加入貼紙、印章與照片，並搜尋過往文字。資料先保存於裝置，並在條件允許時使用 iCloud 私人資料庫備份。免費版包含兩本筆記本；Lifetime 為可選的一次性購買。

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Before you begin

What is Asympta Paperie for?

Paperie is a paper-like digital diary, journal and scrapbook for iPhone and iPad. It is designed for handwritten notes, photos, stickers, stamps and everyday memories.

Can I search old handwritten journal entries?

Typed text is searchable. On supported system versions, Apple Pencil handwriting can also be indexed in the background; indexing depends on device and operating-system support.

Is a subscription required?

No. The free experience includes two notebooks. Optional Paperie Lifetime is a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks unlimited notebooks, without automatic renewal.

How is Paperie different from Asympta Writing?

Paperie provides page-based journals and tactile stationery; Asympta Writing offers an infinite open canvas for visual thinking, handwriting and sketches.
